Regular Classes

Our martial arts classes welcome all skill levels and bring the heat with high-energy sessions focused on technique, fitness, and real-world skills..

Kids Classes

Kids get age-appropriate martial arts training that builds discipline, respect, and core skills — all in a fun, high-energy environment.

Open Mat

Structured open mat time for members to sharpen skills, spar with partners, and get one-on-one tips from coaches.

Private Tuition

Level up with private sessions led by our expert coaches. Book ahead to lock in your ideal time.

Important Notes

  • Please arrive 10-15 minutes before class starts to prepare.
  • Private tuition sessions must be booked at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Membership is required for all regular classes. Day passes are available for visitors.
  • Schedule may change during holidays or special events. Please check our social media for updates.
  • Kids classes are for ages 6-12. Teens (13-17) can join regular classes with parental consent.
