Jiu-Jitsu

A stealthy art of grappling and ground combat, where skill and leverage outmatch brute strength.

Key Benefits:

Karate

A striking art using punching, kicking, knee strikes, elbow strikes and open-hand techniques such as knife-hands.

Key Benefits:
  • Increases strength and power
  • Improves coordination

Judo

A powerful martial art focused on grappling and ground fighting, using technique to outsmart and overpower larger foes.

Key Benefits:
  • Develops balance
  • Improves spatial awareness

Muay Thai

Known as 'The Art of Eight Limbs,' Muay Thai unleashes punches, kicks, elbows, and knees, making full use of eight striking points.

Key Benefits:

  • Enhances striking ability
